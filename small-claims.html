Important reality

Small Claims is simpler than many court processes, but the court still expects proper forms, proof, and organization.

Important things people often do not know

Ontario says there are six stages to a Small Claims Court case: Claim, Default proceedings, Settlement conference, Motions, Trial and judgment, and Enforcement. Ontario also describes the settlement conference as an informal, confidential meeting to explore resolution without the expense and stress of trial. :contentReference[oaicite:12]{index=12}
