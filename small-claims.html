Small Claims Court guidance and preparation

Prepare a cleaner Small Claims Court file.

This section is for money claims, unpaid invoices, damaged property, broken agreements, and other disputes where the key is a clear story, simple proof, and an organized explanation of what you are claiming.

Practical, direct, and focused on simple structure that actually helps.

What usually matters most

  • A simple timeline
  • Receipts, invoices, messages, and photos
  • The amount claimed and why
  • A direct explanation without too much extra detail

Important reality

People often assume Small Claims is casual, but the court still expects organized facts, proper forms, and proof that supports the amount claimed.

Important things people often do not know

Small Claims Court has stages, and one of the important ones is the settlement conference before trial.

Settlement Conference

A standard stage in the case

Used to explore settlement, narrow issues, and avoid unnecessary trial expense where possible.

Plaintiff’s Claim

The case still needs to be set out clearly

You still need a clean explanation of what happened, what amount is claimed, and why the other side is responsible.

Trial Costs

Simple does not mean careless

Parties should still think ahead about whether their evidence and claim are organized enough to justify going further.
