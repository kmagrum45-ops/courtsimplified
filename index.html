Understand the basics

Stronger legal preparation starts with knowing what matters most.

Negligence

What often needs to be shown

  • There was a duty of care
  • Someone failed to act reasonably
  • That failure caused harm
  • The harm can be explained and supported
State Conduct

What often matters

  • A public authority was involved
  • A right or important interest was affected
  • An action or failure is being challenged
  • The impact can be clearly described
Family

What decision-makers often focus on

  • The child’s best interests
  • Safety, stability, and routine
  • Parenting history and reliability
  • Evidence, not just accusation
Civil / Small Claims

What usually helps most

  • A clean timeline
  • Clear facts
  • Supporting documents
  • A clear explanation of the remedy requested

How judges often look at a case

Strong cases are easier to follow because the facts, issues, proof, and requested outcome are laid out clearly. This section helps users understand how to think about their case before they file anything.

What Judges Look For

What usually matters most

  • Clear facts that are easy to follow
  • Evidence that supports the main points
  • Who is legally responsible and why
  • What harm occurred
  • A clear outcome or remedy being requested
Case Structure

How a strong case is usually organized

  • Background of the situation
  • Facts in chronological order
  • The issues that need to be decided
  • The legal concepts that may apply
  • Analysis connecting the facts to the law
  • A conclusion and requested remedy

Frequently asked questions

Clear answers to common questions before getting started.

Is this only for Ontario?

No. CourtSimplified is designed for users across Canada. Ontario currently includes the strongest built-in form guidance.

Does this replace legal advice?

No. CourtSimplified is a legal preparation and organization platform. It is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice.

Can I save my work?

Yes. Your summary can be saved in the browser on the same device so you can return to it later, or saved to your account if you log in.

Can I print my summary?

Yes. You can print or save the generated summary as a PDF from your browser.

Important information

CourtSimplified is a legal preparation and organization platform. It is not a law firm, does not provide legal advice, and does not create a lawyer-client relationship. Users should confirm forms, filing requirements, deadlines, and court rules from official sources before filing anything.
