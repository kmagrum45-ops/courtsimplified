Helpful legal information

Family Law

What courts usually care about

  • Best interests of the child
  • Stability and routine
  • Credibility and consistency
  • Proof, not just accusations
Civil Law

What usually matters most

  • Clear facts and sequence
  • Responsibility and causation
  • Documents, records, and witnesses
  • What outcome is being requested
Small Claims

What helps most

  • A clean timeline
  • Receipts, invoices, messages, and photos
  • The amount claimed and why
  • A simple, organized explanation

Frequently asked questions

Clear answers before people get started.

Does this replace legal advice?

No. CourtSimplified is a legal preparation and organization platform. It is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice.

Can I save my work?

Yes. You can save in the browser or to your account if your database tables are set up in Supabase.

Can I print my summary?

Yes. Use the print button to print or save your summary as a PDF from the browser.

Is payment active?

No. The payment flow is intentionally not active yet, but the plan-selection path is ready for later.

Important information

CourtSimplified is a legal preparation and organization platform. It is not a law firm, does not provide legal advice, and does not create a lawyer-client relationship. Users should confirm forms, filing requirements, deadlines, and court rules from official sources before filing anything.
