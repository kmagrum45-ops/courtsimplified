Legal preparation for people without lawyers

Navigate the justice system with more structure and less confusion.

CourtSimplified is built for people trying to move through court without a lawyer, or before they can afford one. The goal is to turn scattered facts, screenshots, records, and notes into something clearer, more organized, and easier to work with.

Choose Your Case Type Why We Built This

Clearer intake. Better summaries. A stronger starting point.

What CourtSimplified is meant to do

  • Help self-represented people understand the process better
  • Organize facts, timelines, and evidence more professionally
  • Give each case type its own guidance and intake system
  • Create a better foundation before filing or speaking to a lawyer

Who it is for

People handling Family, Civil, and Small Claims matters who need more clarity, more structure, and a more useful starting point than random notes and screenshots.

Why this site exists

Most people do not lose confidence because their case is unimportant. They lose confidence because the system is hard to follow, the forms feel overwhelming, and their evidence is not organized in a way that makes sense.

01

Too much confusion

People often do not know what form they need, what stage they are in, or what a judge will actually focus on.

02

Too much disorder

Important facts get buried inside photos, documents, notes, messages, and screenshots with no clean structure.

03

Too little practical help

Most sites either provide legal information or software. CourtSimplified is designed to connect those two things together.

How it works

Instead of one long page trying to do everything, each case type has its own page with practical information, official links, and a dedicated intake and summary system.

1

3
